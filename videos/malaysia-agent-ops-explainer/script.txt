Malaysia Agent Ops is a tool for Malaysian business workflows that are too important to run from scattered folders, emails, and portal screenshots.

The first focus is halal certification preparation. A business can turn its evidence into a structured dossier, then check whether key documents are present before official submission.

For example, the tool can check company documents, premise evidence, ingredient lists, supplier halal certificates, process flow charts, packaging labels, and expiry dates.

It does not replace JAKIM, and it does not decide what is halal. It helps applicants, consultants, and reviewers see what is ready, what is missing, and what needs human attention.

The second focus is Malaysian tax and e-invoicing. The tool can run a local invoice workflow from submission, to status polling, to payment request, to payment event, until the invoice is paid or blocked.

For real MyInvois submissions, it stops at approval and credential gates. That is intentional. The system should never pretend a live government submission happened.

In plain English, Malaysia Agent Ops is an execution and evidence layer. It helps agents and operators prepare cleaner halal dossiers, run safer e-invoice workflows, and produce audit trails people can inspect.
