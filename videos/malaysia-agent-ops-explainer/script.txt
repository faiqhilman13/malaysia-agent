Malaysia Agent Ops is a practical tool for Malaysian business paperwork that cannot live safely in scattered folders and screenshots.

Start with halal. A manufacturer or restaurant collects documents, and the tool turns them into a structured dossier before official submission.

This is what a passing pre-check looks like. Company registration, PBT licence, product details, ingredient list, supplier halal certificates, process flow, packaging label, and expiry dates are all present.

The OCR checks are there too. The declared supplier name, certificate number, issuing body, and expiry date are compared against what the scanned certificate actually says.

And when something fails, the report should be blunt. An expired certificate, missing supplier evidence, or low-confidence OCR result becomes a fix list before anyone wastes time submitting a weak file.

It does not replace JAKIM, and it does not decide what is halal. It gives applicants, consultants, and reviewers a cleaner evidence trail.

The tax side works the same way. The tool takes an invoice payload, submits it in a sandbox flow, checks status, creates a payment request, ingests the payment event, and marks the invoice paid.

For real MyInvois work, it stops before live risk. No credentials, no explicit approval, no fake government submission.

So in plain English, this is an execution and evidence layer for halal and Malaysian tax operations: cleaner dossiers, safer workflows, and proof people can inspect.
