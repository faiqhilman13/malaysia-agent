01

Why This Wedge

The manual pain sits in evidence collection, supplier verification, renewal discipline, and audit response. Those are machine-friendly, high-frequency workflows even before direct regulator integration exists.

Best Starting Point

Ingredient + Supplier Control

Centralize the upstream supplier and ingredient evidence that currently lives across spreadsheets, PDFs, email threads, and shared drives.

Core Moat

Traceability Graph

Turn BOMs into a compliance graph that connects every product, ingredient, supplier, certificate, and renewal risk in one machine-readable layer.

Commercial Edge

Audit-Speed Advantage

The buyer value is faster submission readiness, fewer missing-evidence loops, and lower dependence on manual compliance staff.

06

First 30 Days

This is the attack plan to turn the halal thesis into a pilot-worthy product wedge.

Week 1-2

Customer + Regulator Discovery

  • Book JAKIM, one MAIN/JAIN, HDC, and at least three manufacturer/OEM interviews.
  • Collect 5 real BOMs, 20 supplier cert samples, and 10 audit query examples.
  • Lock the initial scope to F&B or cosmetics, not both.
Week 2-3

Productization

  • Implement registry, graph, checklist, workflow, and audit workspace in the product.
  • Use one real client dataset to generate an end-to-end evidence pack and export dossier.
  • Measure manual time saved versus the current spreadsheet process.
Week 3-4

Pilot Definition

  • Agree the pilot KPI: at least 50% reduction in manual document handling time.
  • Define the exact submission stage the software supports and where humans still approve.
  • Document every unsupported regulator interaction explicitly.
Never

What Not To Claim

  • Do not claim you are the certifying authority.
  • Do not imply official API access unless it is actually granted.
  • Do not sell "full automation" before you prove the sanctioned boundary.
Position the product as the compliance operating system that prepares, tracks, and explains halal evidence. If official write access is later allowed, that becomes a second-layer advantage, not the first reason the product exists.
