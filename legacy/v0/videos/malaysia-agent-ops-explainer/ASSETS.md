# Explainer Visual Assets

These generated images are project-bound video assets for the Malaysia Agent Ops explainer.

## Files

- `assets/ops-desk-overview.png` -- overall halal and tax operations desk.
- `assets/halal-dossier-precheck.png` -- halal applicant dossier pre-check.
- `assets/ocr-metadata-verification.png` -- OCR and metadata comparison.
- `assets/tax-execution-workflow.png` -- tax invoice execution and payment flow.
- `assets/governance-guardrails.png` -- approval gate and live-provider guardrails.

## Prompt Constraints

The image prompts intentionally avoided embedded text, real government marks, official seals, portal screenshots, human faces, neon dashboards, and fake logos. All precise wording is rendered in HyperFrames overlays instead of inside the generated images.

## Usage

`index.html` uses the images as full-bleed scene backdrops with paper-toned panels. The video is designed to work as a visual explainer without relying on generated voiceover quality.
